        Questions

        Isn't this content free online?

        Online, yes. Blackstart has it downloaded, checked and working with no connection, on any computer.

        How do I open it?

        Plug it in and double-click Open Library (Mac, Windows or Linux). The library opens in your browser. On Android, use the Kiwix app included on the drive.

        Does it need the internet or an account?

        No. No internet, no account, no subscription, no tracking.

        How do updates work?

        When the library is updated, you can download the new version, or have a refreshed drive sent at cost.

        What is the Download version?

        The same library for $49. You bring a 256 GB drive.

        When does it ship?

        Blackstart is made in small batches. The waitlist gets one email when the next batch opens. No payment until then.

        Shipping and returns?

        Ships from the US, free worldwide, tracked. 30-day returns.
